## I build to understand.

My projects begin with a question: How can noisy sensors reveal reliable motion? What makes a game system feel fair? How can a browser coordinate hidden information among a group of players? I answer by prototyping, measuring, revising, and documenting what changed.

This portfolio focuses on the decisions behind the code—not just the finished screen.
